\section*{Spectral Operators}

Spectral operators formalize how local linearizations of operator fields behave
across the reasoning manifold. Spectral signatures encode operator identity,
stability, and drift. Spectral mismatch is one of the primary geometric
indicators of collapse.

Let \( M \) be the reasoning manifold and let \( L(p) : T_pM \rightarrow T_pM \)
denote the local linear operator induced by the operator field at point \( p \).

\subsection*{1. Spectral Bundle}

The spectral bundle is the map:
\[
\mathcal{B}(p) = \mathrm{Spec}(L(p)).
\]

Each fiber of the bundle contains the eigenvalues of the operator at \( p \).
Spectral bundles detect twisting, collapse, and drift across the manifold.

A spectral bundle is stable when:
\[
\mathcal{B}(p) \text{ varies continuously with } p.
\]

Discontinuity indicates operator invention or pseudo-formal constructs.

\subsection*{2. Expected Spectrum}

For each operator field, define an expected spectrum:
\[
\mathrm{Spec}_{\mathrm{expected}}(p).
\]

This expected spectrum is determined by the operator’s field type, modal
context, and geometric neighborhood. It provides the baseline against which
spectral mismatch is measured.

\subsection*{3. Spectral Mismatch}

Spectral mismatch is defined as:
\[
\delta_{\mathrm{spec}}(p) =
\left\| \mathrm{Spec}(L(p)) -
       \mathrm{Spec}_{\mathrm{expected}}(p) \right\|.
\]

Mismatch indicates:

\begin{itemize}
\item operator invention,
\item spectral degeneracy,
\item modal drift,
\item collapse-surface proximity.
\end{itemize}

Spectral mismatch is a primary input to holonomy drift tensors.

\subsection*{4. Spectral Degeneracy}

Spectral degeneracy occurs when eigenvalues collapse or diverge:
\[
\mathrm{Spec}(L(p)) \text{ is degenerate}
\quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad
\exists i \neq j : \lambda_i = \lambda_j
\text{ or }
|\lambda_i| > \theta.
\]

Degeneracy indicates structural instability in the operator field.

\subsection*{5. Operator Identity Drift}

Operator identity drift measures deviation in operator behavior across the
manifold:
\[
D_{\mathrm{op}}(p^-, p^+) =
\left\| L(p^+) - L(p^-) \right\|.
\]

Identity drift is used to detect:

\begin{itemize}
\item operator-field confusion,
\item modal instability,
\item collapse-surface approach.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{6. Spectral Continuity Condition}

Spectral continuity is required for stability:
\[
\left\| \mathcal{B}(p^+) - \mathcal{B}(p^-) \right\| < \epsilon.
\]

Violation indicates that reasoning has left the stable manifold.

\subsection*{7. Spectral Collapse Predicate}

Define:
\[
C_{\mathrm{spec}}(p) =
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{true} & \text{if } \delta_{\mathrm{spec}}(p) > \theta
                 \text{ or } \mathcal{B}(p) \text{ is degenerate}, \\
\mathrm{false} & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

This predicate integrates directly with the unified collapse predicate in
collapse-geometry.tex.
