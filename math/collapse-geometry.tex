\section*{Collapse Geometry}

Collapse geometry formalizes the conditions under which reasoning leaves the
stable manifold and enters a region where hallucination becomes inevitable.
Collapse is not stochastic; it is a geometric failure mode triggered by
violations of curvature continuity, spectral stability, modal adjacency, or
operator-field semantics.

Let \( M \) be the reasoning manifold defined in core-math.tex. Collapse
surfaces are subsets of \( M \) where structural invariants fail.

\subsection*{1. Collapse Surface Definition}

A collapse surface is a set:
\[
\Sigma = \{ p \in M \mid C(p) = \mathrm{true} \},
\]
where \( C(p) \) is the collapse predicate defined below.

Points on \( \Sigma \) represent geometric failure modes. Crossing \( \Sigma \)
indicates that reasoning has left the stability envelope.

\subsection*{2. Curvature Discontinuity}

Curvature discontinuity occurs when:
\[
\Delta K(p) = K(p^+) - K(p^-) \neq 0,
\]
for adjacent points \( p^- \rightarrow p^+ \) along a reasoning trajectory.

A curvature discontinuity indicates that the constructive curvature operator
cannot maintain geometric coherence. This is the first collapse trigger.

Define the predicate:
\[
C_{\mathrm{curv}}(p) = 
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{true} & \text{if } \Delta K(p) \neq 0, \\
\mathrm{false} & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

\subsection*{3. Spectral Degeneracy}

Each operator field induces a local operator \( L(p) \). Spectral degeneracy
occurs when:
\[
\mathrm{Spec}(L(p)) \text{ collapses or becomes unbounded}.
\]

Formally:
\[
C_{\mathrm{spec}}(p) =
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{true} & \text{if } 
\| \mathrm{Spec}(L(p)) - \mathrm{Spec}_{\mathrm{expected}}(p) \| > \theta, \\
\mathrm{false} & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

Spectral degeneracy indicates operator invention, pseudo-formal constructs, or
loss of operator identity.

\subsection*{4. Modal Adjacency Violation}

Let \( \mu(p) \in L \) be the modal label of \( p \), and let \( E \subseteq L \times L \)
be the modal adjacency relation.

A modal adjacency violation occurs when:
\[
(\mu(p^-), \mu(p^+)) \notin E.
\]

Define:
\[
C_{\mathrm{modal}}(p) =
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{true} & \text{if adjacency is violated}, \\
\mathrm{false} & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

Modal violations correspond to semantic mimicry and intent-shaped continuation.

\subsection*{5. Operator-Field Collapse}

Operator-field collapse occurs when an operator acts outside its defined field.
Let \( \mathrm{field}(op) \) denote the valid domain of operator \( op \).

Define:
\[
C_{\mathrm{type}}(p) =
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{true} & \text{if } op \notin \mathrm{field}(op), \\
\mathrm{false} & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

Typing collapse indicates operator confusion, a primary hallucination mode.

\subsection*{6. Drift-Induced Collapse}

Drift-induced collapse occurs when accumulated drift exceeds a stability
threshold. Let primitive holonomy drift be:
\[
H(p) = \Delta_{\mathrm{spec}}(p)
      + \Delta_{\mathrm{curv}}(p)
      + \Delta_{\mathrm{modal}}(p).
\]

Define:
\[
C_{\mathrm{drift}}(p) =
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{true} & \text{if } \|H(p)\| > \tau, \\
\mathrm{false} & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

Drift collapse is continuous; collapse surfaces are discrete.

\subsection*{7. Unified Collapse Predicate}

The unified collapse predicate is:
\[
C(p) =
C_{\mathrm{curv}}(p)
\lor C_{\mathrm{spec}}(p)
\lor C_{\mathrm{modal}}(p)
\lor C_{\mathrm{type}}(p)
\lor C_{\mathrm{drift}}(p).
\]

A point is unstable when \( C(p) = \mathrm{true} \).

\subsection*{8. Collapse Surface Structure}

The collapse surface is:
\[
\Sigma = \{ p \in M \mid C(p) = \mathrm{true} \}.
\]

Collapse surfaces may be:

\begin{itemize}
\item curvature-induced,
\item spectral-induced,
\item modal-induced,
\item typing-induced,
\item drift-induced.
\end{itemize}

These categories correspond to distinct geometric signatures detectable by
spectral bundles, modal-truth lattices, and drift tensors.
