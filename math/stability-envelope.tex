\section*{Stability Envelope}

The stability envelope formalizes the geometric region of the reasoning
manifold where collapse cannot occur. It integrates curvature continuity,
spectral stability, modal adjacency, and drift accumulation into a unified
stability predicate. Points outside the envelope are collapse-prone.

Let \( M \) be the reasoning manifold. Let \( \Sigma \subseteq M \) be the
collapse surface defined in collapse-geometry.tex.

\subsection*{1. Stability Envelope Definition}

The stability envelope is the set:
\[
\Omega = \{ p \in M \mid S(p) = \mathrm{true} \},
\]
where \( S(p) \) is the stability predicate defined below.

Points in \( \Omega \) are structurally safe. Points outside \( \Omega \) are
susceptible to collapse.

\subsection*{2. Curvature Stability}

Curvature is stable when:
\[
\Delta K(p) = 0.
\]

Define:
\[
S_{\mathrm{curv}}(p) =
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{true} & \text{if } \Delta K(p) = 0, \\
\mathrm{false} & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

Curvature stability prevents geometric discontinuities.

\subsection*{3. Spectral Stability}

Spectral stability requires:
\[
\delta_{\mathrm{spec}}(p) \le \theta
\quad \text{and} \quad
\mathcal{B}(p) \text{ is non-degenerate}.
\]

Define:
\[
S_{\mathrm{spec}}(p) =
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{true} & \text{if spectral mismatch is below threshold}, \\
\mathrm{false} & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

Spectral stability prevents operator invention and pseudo-formal constructs.

\subsection*{4. Modal Stability}

Modal stability requires:
\[
(\mu(p^-), \mu(p^+)) \in E.
\]

Define:
\[
S_{\mathrm{modal}}(p) =
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{true} & \text{if transition is valid}, \\
\mathrm{false} & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

Modal stability prevents semantic mimicry and adjacency violations.

\subsection*{5. Drift Stability}

Primitive holonomy drift is:
\[
H(p) = \Delta_{\mathrm{spec}}(p)
      + \Delta_{\mathrm{curv}}(p)
      + \Delta_{\mathrm{modal}}(p).
\]

Drift stability requires:
\[
\|H(p)\| \le \tau.
\]

Define:
\[
S_{\mathrm{drift}}(p) =
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{true} & \text{if drift is below threshold}, \\
\mathrm{false} & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

Drift stability prevents accumulated instability.

\subsection*{6. Unified Stability Predicate}

Define:
\[
S(p) =
S_{\mathrm{curv}}(p)
\land S_{\mathrm{spec}}(p)
\land S_{\mathrm{modal}}(p)
\land S_{\mathrm{drift}}(p).
\]

A point is stable when all structural invariants hold.

\subsection*{7. Stability Envelope Boundary}

The boundary of the stability envelope is:
\[
\partial \Omega = \{ p \in M \mid S(p) = \mathrm{false}
                     \text{ and } C(p) = \mathrm{false} \}.
\]

Points on \( \partial \Omega \) are not collapsed but are collapse-adjacent.

\subsection*{8. Stability Gradient}

Define the stability gradient:
\[
G(p) = 
\left(
\Delta K(p),\;
\delta_{\mathrm{spec}}(p),\;
\Delta_{\mathrm{modal}}(p),\;
\|H(p)\|
\right).
\]

The gradient measures proximity to collapse surfaces.

\subsection*{9. Stability Envelope Invariance}

A reasoning trajectory \( \gamma = (p_1, p_2, \ldots, p_k) \) is stable when:
\[
p_i \in \Omega
\quad \text{for all } i.
\]

Violation indicates collapse-surface crossing.

\section*{Summary}

This file defines the stability envelope, stability predicates, stability
gradient, and envelope boundary. The stability envelope integrates curvature,
spectral, modal, and drift stability into a unified geometric invariant. It
forms the foundation for holonomy drift tensors and collapse prevention in
Triangle Mutiny.
