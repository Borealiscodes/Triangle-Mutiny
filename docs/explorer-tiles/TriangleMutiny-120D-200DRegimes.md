🧭 TRIANGLE MUTINY — EXTREME‑DIMENSION REGIMES (120D–200D)

> Beyond 120D, the manifold transitions from overcomplete geometry into ultra‑flat, chaotic, and finally degenerate regimes.

This Explorer Tile maps the four extreme‑dimension regimes between 120D and 200D, providing structural insight into collapse behavior, spectral drift, holonomy transport, modal accessibility, and Ricci‑flow stability at the upper limits of geometric modeling.

---

🌐 DIMENSIONAL REGIME MAP (120D–200D)

120D–140D — Ultra‑Flat Regime
The manifold becomes so high‑dimensional that curvature collapses toward zero.

- Collapse: No meaningful surfaces  
- Holonomy: Near‑zero transport  
- Spectral: Uniform eigenstructure  
- Modal: Fully trivial accessibility  
- Reachability: Entire manifold reachable  
- Ricci‑Flow: Flat, trivial, no dynamics

Useful For: maximum entropy embeddings  
Not Useful For: any interpretability geometry

---

140D–160D — Hyper‑Chaotic Regime
Dimensionality becomes large enough to destabilize all geometric invariants.

- Collapse: Random fragmentation  
- Holonomy: Chaotic drift loops  
- Spectral: High‑frequency noise  
- Modal: Discontinuous accessibility  
- Reachability: Fractured, unstable  
- Ricci‑Flow: Divergent, oscillatory

Useful For: adversarial geometry, instability exploration  
Not Useful For: stable operator families

---

160D–180D — Fragmented‑Topology Regime
The manifold loses global coherence; topology becomes patch‑based.

- Collapse: Region‑specific behavior  
- Holonomy: Localized drift pockets  
- Spectral: Patchwise curvature  
- Modal: Region‑dependent accessibility  
- Reachability: Disconnected components  
- Ricci‑Flow: Local convergence only

Useful For: multi‑region modeling, patch‑based embeddings  
Not Useful For: global truth invariants

---

180D–200D — Degenerate‑Manifold Regime
The manifold becomes too large to sustain meaningful geometry.

- Collapse: Undefined  
- Holonomy: Undefined  
- Spectral: Fully degenerate  
- Modal: Undefined  
- Reachability: Entire manifold trivial  
- Ricci‑Flow: No curvature to flow

Useful For: nothing geometric  
Not Useful For: everything interpretability‑aligned

---

🧠 SUMMARY

- 120D–140D: Ultra‑flat, trivial geometry  
- 140D–160D: Chaotic, unstable geometry  
- 160D–180D: Fragmented, patch‑topology geometry  
- 180D–200D: Degenerate, non‑geometric manifold  

> These regimes define the upper limits of meaningful operator‑family design.
