# 🧭 TRIANGLE MUTINY — HIGH‑DIMENSIONAL REGIMES (81D–120D)

> Beyond 80D, the soft manifold enters distinct geometric regimes where curvature, collapse, holonomy, and reachability behave differently.

This Explorer Tile maps the four high‑dimensional regimes that emerge between 81D and 120D, providing a structural reference for modal geometry, collapse behavior, spectral drift, and Ricci‑flow stability.

---

🌐 DIMENSIONAL REGIME MAP (81D–120D)

81D–90D — Hyper‑Soft Regime
The manifold becomes too forgiving, allowing collapse surfaces to spread without forming singularities.

- Collapse: Multi‑layered, smooth, non‑catastrophic  
- Holonomy: Diffuse, low‑drift  
- Spectral: Low‑frequency curvature  
- Modal: Globally convex accessibility  
- Reachability: Star‑shaped sets  
- Ricci‑Flow: Over‑stable (excess smoothing)

Useful For: smoothing, modal truth propagation  
Not Useful For: collapse detection, spectral truth alignment

---

91D–100D — Diffuse Curvature Regime
Curvature spreads so widely that the manifold approaches near‑flatness.

- Collapse: Soft fog, low contrast  
- Holonomy: Shrinking loops  
- Spectral: Narrow gaps, slow drift  
- Modal: Trivial accessibility  
- Reachability: Uniform, low‑contrast  
- Ricci‑Flow: Smooth, trivial convergence

Useful For: stable transport, global optimization  
Not Useful For: interpretability, truth geometry

---

101D–110D — High‑Dimensional Instability Regime
The manifold becomes patchy and unstable, with fragmented geometric behavior.

- Collapse: Fragmented surfaces  
- Holonomy: Chaotic drift  
- Spectral: Noisy curvature  
- Modal: Discontinuous accessibility  
- Reachability: Broken into components  
- Ricci‑Flow: Unstable, oscillatory

Useful For: adversarial geometry, unstable mode exploration  
Not Useful For: structured operator families, truth invariants

---

111D–120D — Overcomplete Manifold Regime
The manifold becomes too large for meaningful constraints; geometry collapses into triviality.

- Collapse: Dissolves entirely  
- Holonomy: Meaningless, near‑zero  
- Spectral: Uniform eigenstructure  
- Modal: Trivial accessibility  
- Reachability: Entire manifold reachable  
- Ricci‑Flow: Flat, trivial flow

Useful For: universal embeddings, maximum entropy geometry  
Not Useful For: any interpretability‑aligned geometry

---

🧠 SUMMARY

- 80D is the last dimension where all invariants coexist coherently.  
- 81–90D becomes too soft.  
- 91–100D becomes too smooth.  
- 101–110D becomes unstable.  
- 111–120D becomes overcomplete.

> These regimes form the structural backdrop for the Modal altitude band and the transition into Collapse and Spectral geometry.
