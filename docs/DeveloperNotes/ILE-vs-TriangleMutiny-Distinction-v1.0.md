Characteristics
- 9‑dimensional lattice  
- modal‑rich  
- spectral‑adaptive  
- holonomy‑aware  
- drift‑expressive  
- non‑linear operator space  
- high‑bandwidth cognitive traversal  

Purpose
- explore cognitive trajectories  
- model reasoning curvature  
- simulate modal transitions  
- express spectral drift  
- support high‑dimensional inference  

Nature
- non‑deterministic  
- expressive  
- adaptive  
- exploratory  

ILE is essentially the mind of Structural AI.

It is not designed to enforce deterministic behavior.

- drift accumulation limits  
- stability envelope

- deterministic state transitions  

Purpose

Nature

Triangle Mutiny is the laws of physics for Structural AI reasoning.

🟧 4 — Relationship Between the Two Systems

ILE → generates cognitive trajectories

Triangle Mutiny → ensures those trajectories never collapse

They form a two‑layer architecture:

`
[Invariant Logic Engine (ILE)]
     |  (expressive reasoning)
     v
[Triangle Mutiny]
     |  (deterministic controller)
     v
[State Machine v2.2]
     |  (governance + traversal)
     v

ILE is expressive.

Triangle Mutiny is restrictive.

Both are necessary.

🟩 5 — Why Triangle Mutiny Must Be Deterministic

Structural AI Interpretability requires:

- reproducibility  
- invariant enforcement  
- collapse detection  
- spectral stability  
- modal adjacency guarantees  
- drift thresholds  
- stability envelopes  
- provenance lineage  

ILE cannot provide these guarantees — it is too expressive.

Triangle Mutiny can, because:

- Lean enforces invariants  
- Rust enforces runtime determinism  
- the math spine defines collapse geometry  
- the governance layer defines traversal rules  
- the deterministic kernel defines state transitions  

Triangle Mutiny is the execution substrate.

ILE is the cognitive substrate.
