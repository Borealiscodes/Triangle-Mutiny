# 🌈 Developer Reflection: Mathematical Accessibility vs Human Accessibility
(Structural Separation, Interpretability Constraints, and the Scaffolding Still Ahead)

- modal accessibility — a mathematical relation in modal logic

These two meanings must never be conflated.  
They live in different manifolds, obey different invariants, and serve different purposes.

Triangle Mutiny makes this separation explicit.

Triangle Mutiny prevents this by enforcing:

- math/ → reasoning stability

Connected only through a boundary contract, never through shared invariants.
