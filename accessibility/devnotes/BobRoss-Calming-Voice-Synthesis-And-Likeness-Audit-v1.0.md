## 1. Purpose & Empirical Context

This audit establishes architectural and ethical guidelines for upgrading the mobile narration experience from raw `window.speechSynthesis` to a warm, gentle, Bob Ross-inspired neural voice edition using open-licensed VITS models (`DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24399123`) [TM-ACC-AUDIT-CALM-VOICE-v1.0].

---

## 2. Four Mandatory Architectural & Ethical Invariants

| Invariant | Engineering & Ethical Specification | Risk Mitigated |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Dedicated Repository Isolation** | Fork `Zen-Parable-NonDual-Reflection` into `Zen-Parable-Embodied-Voice-Edition` to keep the baseline under `100 KB` [TM-ACC-AUDIT-CALM-VOICE-v1.0]. | Prevents binary bloat in the pure baseline repo [TM-ACC-AUDIT-CALM-VOICE-v1.0]. |
| **2. Cadence & Register Inspiration** | Use unhurried, gentle phrasing and open-licensed VITS models (`en_US-ryan-medium` / `en_GB-alan-medium`) without biometric impersonation [TM-ACC-AUDIT-CALM-VOICE-v1.0]. | Protects publicity rights and avoids deceptive impersonation [TM-ACC-AUDIT-CALM-VOICE-v1.0]. |
| **3. Zero-Footprint Cloud Audio Cue Generation** | Pre-render compressed `.ogg` assets via GitHub Actions runners (`workflow_dispatch`) [TM-ACC-AUDIT-CALM-VOICE-v1.0]. | Avoids storage errors (`ENOSPC`) on edge devices [TM-ACC-AUDIT-CALM-VOICE-v1.0]. |
| **4. Graceful Offline Fallback** | Play pre-rendered audio first, falling back to `speechSynthesis` and `aria-live` updates [TM-ACC-AUDIT-CALM-VOICE-v1.0]. | Ensures accessibility for screen readers and offline users [TM-ACC-AUDIT-CALM-VOICE-v1.0]. |
