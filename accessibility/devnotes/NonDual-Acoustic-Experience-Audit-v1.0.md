## 1. The Engineering Failure Mode: Post-Hoc Screen Readers as Structural Dualism

Testing `Zen-Parable-Audio-Sanctuary` revealed a subtle, critical flaw in conventional game accessibility engineering:

* **What We Built First:** A 2D `(x, y)` coordinate canvas (`640 × 380 px`) with a bolted-on `aria-live` status readout announcing distances and compass bearings (e.g., *"Solitary Toro Lantern: east, 37% proximity"*).
* **Why It Failed Phenomenologically:** While technically navigable via keyboard and screen reader, **perception, physical action, and philosophical meaning remained severed**. The player was forced to operate an external coordinate-chasing mini-game while an out-of-band narrator barked telemetry—reproducing the exact **dualist split** critiqued in `DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24240994` (separating generation from post-hoc verification) and `koan-interpretations-guide.md` (dividing subject from object).

When a screen reader merely translates visual coordinates into text after the fact, blind and low-vision players receive a *description of a visual puzzle* rather than inhabiting a unified aesthetic whole (*rasa-darśan* / perceptual-cognitive unity).

## 2. Why the RNIB / Roundhouse / Patrick Ness Sensory Experience Worked

Examining the **RNIB and Roundhouse sensory-darkness storytelling collaborations** (such as Patrick Ness's immersive audio-and-tactile performances in total darkness at London's Roundhouse) reveals three architectural principles that dissolve the sighted-vs.-blind divide:

1. **Shared Primary Medium (Acoustic & Tactile First):** Sighted and blind participants inhabit the **exact same sensory field**—live spatial sound, spoken narrative, breath, airflow, and tactile texture in darkness. There is no "primary visual movie" with a secondary audio-description patch glued onto the side.
2. **Co-Presence of Voice and Environment:** The storyteller's voice is woven directly into the living acoustic environment (river water, wind gusts, resonant bronze) rather than interrupting play like a cold system telemetry alert.
3. **Contemplative Pacing Over Mechanical Optimization:** Stripping away visual clutter and coordinate-chasing slows down frantic button-mashing, inviting the participant to sit inside tension, ambiguity, and stillness.

## 3. Architectural Shift: From Coordinate Chasing to Embodied Non-Dual Gestures (`Zen-Parable-NonDual-Reflection`)

To honor both the **RNIB Roundhouse model** and the **Zen teaching parables**, the next iteration (`Zen-Parable-NonDual-Reflection`) eliminates 2D `(x, y)` pixel navigation entirely. Instead, **every physical key/touch gesture, acoustic response, and spoken poetic reflection occurs as one unified event**:

## 4. Audit Invariants for Future Accessibility Design (`Triangle-Mutiny` & `ndh-game-dev-kit`)

1. **No Bolted-On Coordinate Telemetry for Contemplative Works:** When designing narrative or contemplative experiences, never force blind/low-vision users to navigate a 2D pixel grid via `aria-live` distance readouts; design gestures directly around unified sensory states.
2. **Perceptual-Cognitive Unity (`Rasa-Darśan`):** Voice narration, procedural soundscape, and deterministic `Q16.16` state transitions must fire together as a single coherent beat rather than competing channels.
3. **Reflective Transparency:** Surface the philosophical and engineering tension openly in a companion reflection panel so players can examine *how* the interface shapes attention.
